staging area. Its six source QIIME/BIOM files and merged representative
sequences were byte-for-byte duplicates of the canonical copies in
`data/3Countries/` and were removed during cleanup.

`merge_preprocess.py` and the empty `China.tsv` are retained only as historical
context; use the current study folders for active work.
